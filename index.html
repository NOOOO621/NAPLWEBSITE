The NAPL is a community hockey league that lives on Discord. Teams have owners, rosters and a schedule. Games are played live, with broadcasters calling them, and a champion is crowned at the end. Season One is 3v3, with six teams playing on Friday nights and Saturdays.

How it works

Game day

  • Games are 3 periods of 5 minutes, 15 minutes of regulation.
  • Check the schedule and be in the lobby on time.
  • There is a 10-minute grace period. After that a team that can't field a lineup forfeits, and a forfeit is a 3–0 loss.
  • The winning side reports the score in the league channel.

Standings

  • Regulation win: 3 points
  • Overtime win: 2 points
  • Overtime loss: 1 point
  • Regulation loss or forfeit: 0 points

Playoffs

The league runs a single-elimination playoff. Lose and you're out, win and you move on. The last team standing is the NAPL champion, and league awards like MVP and Best Goalkeeper go out at the end.

Players

Join a team's roster and show up for your games. Rosters are flexible, but a team must be able to field a full lineup. Players get onto teams through the draft and through trades and free agency.

Owners

Owners run a team. They keep the roster up to date, recruit players, pass along game times and make sure their team shows up.

Broadcasters

Broadcasters call the games for the league. They show up before the match, use the right team names and scores, and keep the commentary respectful.

Want in?

Ask in the NAPL Discord to join as a player, owner or broadcaster. Everyone follows the official rulebook, and the short version is simple: be respectful, show up on time, and play fair.
